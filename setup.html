ZEBESTERMINAL

Connect Twitch chat

Turn your BRB scene into a Super Metroid quiz. Viewers answer in Twitch chat and earn a place on your stream’s leaderboard.

Customize font The default is Zebes Relay. To use your own, link directly to a webfont that allows cross-origin loading.

Super Metroid · A B C D · 01:59

Add it to OBS

  1. Add a Browser source to your BRB scene.
  2. Paste your link into URL. Set the size to 1920 × 1080.
  3. Switch to the scene. Chat’s first A, B, C or D per viewer counts for that question.

Scores and answer history stay in that OBS browser profile across streams. To browse a viewer’s answers or export the log, choose Interact in OBS, press V and open Scorebook.
